The shop

DREAMZ DC Compound is a Washington DC cannabis dispensary built around lab-tested flower, vapes, edibles, and an in-house DREAMZ COMPOUND line. Walk in after you self-certify, or order online for delivery and curbside.

We keep the menu tight on purpose: recognizable brands, clear potency data, and staff who can point you to the right strain instead of a wall of mystery jars.
