FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHERE IS DREAMZ DC LOCATED?

DREAMZ DC is located in Washington, DC. Our dispensary serves the entire DC metro area with in-store pickup and fast delivery.

WHAT ARE THE OPERATING HOURS?

Open 7 days a week! Sunday: 11AM–10PM, Monday–Thursday: 10AM–12AM, Friday–Saturday: 10AM–2AM. We're open for Labor Day and all major holidays.

DO I NEED A MEDICAL CARD?

No medical card? No problem! DC's Medical Cannabis Program is now OPEN to non-residents and international visitors 21+. Click here to register for an instant Visitor Pass.

DO YOU OFFER DELIVERY?

Yes! We offer fast, discreet delivery throughout Washington DC. Average delivery time is under 1 hour. Select delivery at checkout.

ARE PRODUCTS LAB-TESTED?

Absolutely. All DREAMZ DC products are lab-tested for potency, purity, and safety. We provide full cannabinoid profiles and terpene data for every product.

HOW DO I START SHOPPING WITHOUT A MEDICAL CARD?

Adults 21+ can self-certify instantly at our self-certification portal. No doctor's visit required. Shop hundreds of products immediately after registering.

WHAT MAKES DREAMZ DC UNIQUE?

DREAMZ DC offers a curated selection of premium strains, exclusive DREAMZ COMPOUND branded products, competitive pricing, and fast delivery. We're more than a dispensary — we're the destination for cannabis enthusiasts in DC.

CAN I PLACE AN ORDER FOR PICKUP?

Yes! Place your order online and pick it up at our DC location. We offer in-store pickup, kiosk pickup, and delivery.
